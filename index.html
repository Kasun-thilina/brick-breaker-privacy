Privacy Policy — Brick Breaker: Survival

Effective date: 5 July 2026 · App: Brick Breaker: Survival (com.kasuncreations.brickbreaker) · Developer: Kasun Creations

This policy explains what information the Brick Breaker: Survival mobile game ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The short version: the app collects no name, email, or contact details from you; game progress is stored on your device; ads and optional online features (leaderboard, crash reporting) involve the third-party services described below.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, high scores), settings (sound, music, haptics, analytics preference), and consent choices are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. Deleting the app removes them.

3. Advertising consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can decline personalized ads and still play; rewarded ads remain available where required for optional in-game rewards. On iOS, the app additionally asks for App Tracking Transparency permission; if you decline, ads are non-personalized. You can change your consent choice at any time from the app's settings.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Analytics & crash reports: toggle off in Settings → Privacy → "Analytics & Crash Reports".
  • Play Games / Game Center: optional; connect or ignore from the in-app prompt or Settings.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Privacy → "Delete My Data" erases your scores, profile and leaderboard entries from our servers and your device. You can also email us (below) to request deletion.
  • Ad personalization: manage via the in-app consent options, your device's ad settings (reset/limit ad ID), or iOS tracking permissions.
  • If you are in the EEA/UK, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection under the GDPR; California residents have equivalent rights under the CCPA. Contact us to exercise them.

5. Data retention

Leaderboard entries are kept until you delete them (in-app or by request). Analytics and crash data are retained by Google Firebase according to its standard retention periods (up to 14 months for analytics). Local data lives only on your device.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where consent signals indicate an underage user, ads are served in non-personalized mode. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Online features use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Leaderboard data is protected by server-side rules that only allow each player to write or delete their own entry.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.
