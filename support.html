Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?

Open the in-game Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID (App Store) or Google account you originally bought it with — the purchase is tied to that store account, so it restores for free on any device.

I removed ads but still see rewarded ads — is that a bug?

No. "Remove Ads" removes the banner and full-screen ads between games. The optional rewarded ads — the ones you choose to watch for a Survival revive, a Classic continue, or an extra ball — stay available to everyone. If you've removed ads, those rewards are granted without watching an ad.

How do revives and continues work?

In Survival, you can revive up to 3 times per run (it clears the bottom row so you can keep going). In Classic, you can continue up to 2 times per level. Both are optional and, for players who've removed ads, are granted instantly.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete My Data. This erases your scores, profile and leaderboard entries from your device and our servers. You can also email us to request deletion. (Purchases live on your store account and can be restored later.)

I reinstalled the app and my progress is gone.

Level progress and high scores are stored on your device, so uninstalling clears them. To keep your leaderboard identity across devices and reinstalls, connect Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) from the first-launch prompt or Settings → Profile → Connect.

My name isn't showing on the leaderboard.

Set a player name in Settings, and optionally connect Play Games / Game Center so your name and avatar sync. Leaderboard features need an internet connection.

How do I turn off sound, music, haptics, or analytics?

All of these are in Settings. Sound, music and haptics are under Audio & Feedback; the analytics & crash-report opt-out is under Privacy.

Found a bug or have a suggestion?

We'd love to hear it. Email kasuncreations1@gmail.com and, if it's a bug, let us know your device model and what happened — it helps us fix it faster.
